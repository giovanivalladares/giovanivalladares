I've worked heavily in two psychology labs, where I run studies, collect and code data, and analyze it in **R** and **Python**. ## Research Experience

### PSYCHE Lab · University of Denver
**Research Assistant** · August 2025 – Present

- Studying self-guided interventions done before and/or after a psilocybin experience, and whether they can enhance psychological insight and well-being and reduce existential anxiety
- Administer questionnaires and interviews, and code responses, across a sample of **80 participants**
- Currently working as co-first author on a preliminary analysis manuscript
- Work with participants who have recently used high doses of psilocybin

### ACCTION Lab · University of Denver
**Research Assistant** · September 2026 – Present

- Working on the Culturally Responsive Assessments for Teens (CRAFT) project
- Developed Revised QUAD for Parents and Teen participants to measure stigma related to teens with ADHD
- Currently in training to conduct clinical interviews with participants

### RISE Lab · University of Denver
**C-BEC Research Assistant** · September 2024 – March 2025

- Collaborated on the C-BEC project, analyzing teacher bias with students of color
- Collected data with **5 partner schools**
- Collected and analyzed 25-minute sessions of real-time physiological data (BIOPAC) from teachers watching videos of students misbehaving

### Poster Presentation
